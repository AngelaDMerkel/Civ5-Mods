# Irrigated Farm

Turn connected stretches of flat desert into productive farmland. Build
**Irrigation Works**, start beside a natural water source, then extend your farms
outward. Protect the connecting farms: a broken link can dry out the whole branch.

- Use rivers or nearby mountains, lakes and oases as water sources.
- Gain **+3 food from each supplied farm improvement**, falling to +1 when dry.
- Restore irrigation by repairing or reconnecting damaged farms.

**Currently included with Realistic Terrain v2.** Requires Brave New World and a
new single-player game; in-game testing is pending. Terraced hills and the custom
farm artwork are planned additions, not current playable features.
