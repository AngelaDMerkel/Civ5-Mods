# Climate Change: planned features

[Mod collection](../../../README.md) · [Current playable features](../../../docs/GAMEPLAY.md)

Climate Change is intended to make Civilization V's landscape evolve over the
course of a game. Conditions that shaped an early settlement may change by the
industrial era, creating new reasons to adapt your empire's land use.

**This mod is in planning. There is no playable build, installation package or
gameplay screenshot yet.** The other mods in this collection do not currently
activate a global climate system.

## The agreed direction

Each game will have one lasting climate direction: **warming or cooling**.
Its pace may vary, but the long-term trend will not repeatedly reverse.

Natural change throughout history will be part of the system. Industrial-era
human activity and the influence of larger city populations will also matter.
Exactly how these factors combine is still being designed.

## What may change on the map

Terrain transitions, vegetation changes and sea-ice changes are being explored.
For example, snow becoming tundra is a possible warming effect. Specific transition
chains, warnings, timing and effects on yields are not finalized promises.

Visible changes need to be checked in the game as well as changes to tile yields.
Coastal flooding, city relocation and destructive land-to-water changes are not
confirmed features. Do not plan a current game around them.

## Units, buildings and player controls

No new unit, building, worker action or climate-control panel has been finalized.
There is no current button to enable warming, prevent cooling or protect a city.
The eventual guide will explain those choices once they exist.

Until then, [Realistic Terrain](../../realistic-terrain/docs/GUIDE.md) handles
military exposure to the terrain already on the map, while
[Irrigated Farm](../../irrigated-farm/docs/GUIDE.md) handles farm water connections.
Neither should be read as a completed Climate Change feature.
