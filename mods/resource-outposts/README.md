# Resource Outposts

Claim strategic and luxury resources beyond your borders without founding a city.
Send a worker to a neutral deposit, build an outpost, and decide whether its
resources are worth the upkeep and the troops needed to defend it.

- Gain access to distant resources without expanding your territory.
- Fortify a defender with the outpost's **+50% defensive bonus**.
- Locate, manage, abandon or convert sites from one overview.
- Use outposts independently, or combine them with Terrain for garrison shelter.

**Experimental v1 build.** Requires Brave New World and a new single-player game;
in-game testing is pending. No roads, Ministry of Coin or Terrain mod are required.
