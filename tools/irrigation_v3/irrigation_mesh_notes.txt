IRRIGATED FARM — SOURCE MESH REVIEW v3
9 October 2026. Unapproved revision; no native game integration.

WHAT CHANGED
This revision responds to the overly geometric v1/v2 meshes and sparse water
network. The farmed slopes now use partial, uneven contour benches, exposed
unworked shoulders, dry gullies and low retaining walls. The terraces do not
form complete concentric hexagonal rings. Terrain normals are smoothed while
retaining masonry stays hard edged. Different tree scales, painted foliage
cutouts, granular crop textures and smaller masonry replace the repeated solid
canopies, mathematical crop sprouts and large clean wall panels.

The original atlas was generated with the built-in imagegen tool. The source
image is 1254 square; it is packed to 2048 square for the exports. Packing does
not add source detail. The original and exact prompt are preserved under
shared/art/irrigated-farm/mesh-v3-review/ and copied into this kit's source/.
provenance.json records the source hashes and unapproved review status. No
extracted Firaxis textures or meshes are included in the source kit.

WATER NETWORK
Each hill has a high receiver, descending lined supply cuts, contour channels
on level benches, and small branches running outward into the crop beds. Short
aqueducts carry contour channels across dry gullies. Flat farms have a supply
channel, several secondary headers, and many narrow bed channels. Junction
banks are notched at branch mouths. Every route has an upstream route ID.

The connected district has one source. Hills receive water at their upper
intakes. Flat farms receive water from the lowest contour channels of their
upstream hills, rather than from a giant inclined bridge starting at a summit.
Waterways retain a level or falling waterline. Drops remain high until beyond
the upper retaining edge, then descend into the lower bench. High aqueducts are
rigid spans and must never be terrain-warped into a dip followed by a climb.

Validation checks route reachability and exact junction positions, monotone
water elevations, and bed clearance over the full channel width. These are
source geometry checks, not a flow-rate, pressure, discharge or fluid simulation.
The flow review is an annotation of the exported routes on an actual GLB render.

SOURCE CONTRACT AND OUTPUTS
Z up, radius-six hex footprints, normalized authoring units. Native units,
placement and scale have not been calibrated. Six shared edge directions and
64 neighbor masks remain in connectors.json. That inventory is not a native
runtime system for dynamic per-edge mesh placement.

The 36 outputs include four terrain families, wet/dry/construction/pillaged
states, wet LOD1 variants, separate connectors, vegetation and a district.
OBJ/MTL, textured GLB, and rigid-root BR2 are provided. The GLB material uses
alpha masking; the OBJ MTL includes map_d. Native alpha/material binding remains
to be configured and verified during GR2 conversion. Neutral normal/specular
DDS files are placeholders. The interactive viewer has simpler lighting than
the static preview renderer.

LOD1 reduces plant geometry and hidden channel structure; it preserves water
routes, edge positions and terrain boundaries. Triangle counts are measured
from these exports, not asserted to match native Civ V budgets. In-game
performance and further native LOD optimization remain untested.

Crop texture coordinates use a common world frame. Where cultivated surfaces
meet across tile edges, field species and UV phase agree. Partial benches may
meet unworked shoulders, so not every edge location has crops on both sides.
The preview terrain is authored context; fitting to actual Civ V terrain is
still required. The generic cone mountain was removed from the review scene;
the elevated source socket represents an attachment to a native mountain.

LIMITS
This is an art and hydraulic-layout review, not an installable mod. No native
GR2 conversion, game installation, dynamic native adjacency, terrain fitting,
or in-game art acceptance has occurred. Current playable irrigation remains
flat-desert gameplay using normal Farm art. Source-only visual refinements do
not change that implementation. Native conversion reference:
  https://github.com/deliverator23/Civilization-Blender-Scripts

REBUILD
Requires Python 3, NumPy and Pillow.
  python tools/irrigation_v3/build_irrigation_meshes.py
  python tools/irrigation_v3/validate_irrigation_meshes.py
  python tools/irrigation_v3/render_flow_review.py
From the source bundle:
  python source/build_irrigation_meshes.py --output rebuilt
  python source/validate_irrigation_meshes.py --output rebuilt
Use --quick to skip static renders. Validation results are in validation.json.
