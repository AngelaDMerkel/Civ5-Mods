Aqueduct side profile

The steepest exported span measures 0.8% grade (about 0.46°), below the 2% design limit. Approaches turn gradually into the receiving channels. Short masonry receiving chutes absorb the remaining height difference. These are model design limits, not a calibrated simulation of the game terrain.
