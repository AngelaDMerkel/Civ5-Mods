IRRIGATED FARM — MODULAR SOURCE MESH KIT v1
Based on the user's approved terraced-terrain concept, 9 October 2026.

DELIVERY
36 original assets in OBJ/MTL, textured GLB and rigid-root BR2 formats:
  20 farm meshes: Flat, GentleHill, SteepHill, RockyFoothill, each with Wet,
     Dry, Construction and Pillaged states, plus a lower-detail Wet version.
  4 channel pieces: straight, 60-degree bend, downward drop and three-way fork.
  6 aqueducts: Short, Long and Mountain spans, each at two detail levels.
  1 mountain intake, 4 vegetation props (two date-palm and two fruit-tree LODs).
  1 connected six-tile demonstration assembly.
The assembly includes simple reference rock geometry. It is context only,
not a proposed replacement for Civ V's native mountains.

Open index.html in a browser. It is self-contained and works offline.
The viewer reads the same vertex/normal/UV arrays as the GLB exports. It supports
orbit, zoom, wireframe, untextured geometry, vegetation and water visibility.
PNG reviews are software renders of the exported GLB triangles and textures,
not generated concept illustrations. Review both the individual tiles and the
connected district before converting.

CIV V ART DIRECTION AND REFERENCES
The design follows the native farm's broad crop patches and the Incan terrace
farm's angular planted shelves, low retaining walls and subdued materials.
Crop rows are diffuse texture detail, not individual high-poly plants. Date
palms use narrow opaque frond ribbons; fruit trees use small faceted canopies.
The kit has one original 1024x1024 diffuse atlas. It contains no extracted
Firaxis geometry or pixels. The atlas and renders were made procedurally from
the actual source meshes. The neutral normal/specular DDS files are source
placeholders; they do not claim a validated native Civ V shader setup.

Real-game references examined directly:
  Medieval_Farm.fxsxml in Resource/Common/ImprovementModels.fpk references
  Ancient_Farm_Decal_D/H, Ancient_Farm_Diff/SREF and Generic_Grey_8.
  Incan_Terrace_Farm.fxsxml in Resource/Common/ConquestDeluxeModels.fpk
  references Incan_Terrace_DIFF, Incan_Terrace_SREF and Generic_Grey_8.
  The installed Incan_Terrace_DIFF is 512x512. Its crop stripe and stone palette
  informed the original materials, rather than being redistributed.
  Civ5ArtDefines_Landmarks_DLC_02.xml: terrace farm uses SNAPSHOT,
  bTerrainContour=True and fScale=1.0. This is a reference, not evidence that a

GEOMETRY CONTRACT
Z-up source coordinates; origin at the hex center on the lowest cultivated
surface. Hex circumradius = 6.0, apothem = 3*sqrt(3), width across flats =
6*sqrt(3). These are normalized authoring units, NOT measured Civ V engine
units. Native scale, placement and height offsets must be calibrated in game.

Edge order is E, NE, NW, W, SW, SE. Each edge's center is a snap anchor, with
60-degree rotations. Opposite anchors coincide when two tile centers are
separated by twice the apothem. All connectors use water width 0.24, rim width
0.085 and water depth 0.07. Open channel ends are deliberate snap interfaces.

The base tiles contain crop overlays, terraces, vegetation and an internal
downhill distribution chute. They DO NOT contain a sealed hex pedestal or
perimeter wall. Crop rows use world-aligned parcel coordinates. The district
assembly regenerates clipped parcels using each tile's world origin, so UV
phase and crop species match across shared edges. Do not rotate a baked crop
overlay independently of that common parcel grid.

The 64 possible neighbor masks are listed in connectors.json. They reduce to
14 rotational classes; mask zero is included. This inventory is a source-art
connection contract, not 64 automatically functioning native improvement
models. The span/route builder accepts coordinates and rejects uphill routes.
All six orientations still require real terrain-height and water-grade data
when choosing and placing pieces in an engine implementation.

Rigid aqueducts and terrain-adapted farm overlays are separate assets. The
waterline is straight/graded over a gap; arch supports respond to terrain.
Do not terrain-warp the whole aqueduct and reintroduce the dip-then-climb
failure. Slope drops are lined chutes, not large waterfalls. Retaining walls
have openings at the terrace distribution chute.

The connected assembly has one source and a directed feeder tree to every
tile's highest inlet. Each tile distributes to lower shelves. Tests sample
the full outer width of the feeder bed against the terraced surfaces and
check water elevations for monotonic descent. These checks concern this
source demonstration, not arbitrary native terrain or a hydraulic simulation.

MODEL ORGANIZATION
OBJ groups / GLB extras / per-model JSON identify crop surfaces, retaining
walls, coping, channel beds/rims/water, arch piers/spandrels/intrados/decks,
vegetation, and state-specific construction or repair props.
Each BR2 has one World bone, complete rigid weights, UV-derived tangents and
bitangents. BR2 and OBJ are Z-up. GLB applies a root rotation for standard
Y-up viewers. Do not apply that GLB root transform twice when round-tripping.
LOD1 reduces vegetation/frond and arch segmentation while preserving terrace
boundaries and connector endpoints. These are authoring LODs; native automatic
LOD selection is not configured.

NATIVE CONVERSION AND LIMITS
1. Inspect/import OBJ or GLB in a modeler and calibrate the normalized tile
   scale against an actual native improvement. The binary GR2 meshes were not
   decoded, so the triangle budgets are chosen design targets, not claimed
   measurements of Firaxis's meshes.
2. Import BR2 via the established Nexus Buddy workflow on a supported setup.
   Bind the material/shader and DDS textures inside the native GR2 asset.
   Listing a texture in FXSXML alone does not establish native material binding.
3. Export GR2 and define FXSXML and landmark states. Fit farm surfaces to the
   terrain separately from the rigid aqueducts. Validate normals, UV origin,
   shadows, scale and source/connector elevations in Civ V.
4. Implement/update the improvement placement and art-selection logic. The
   current playable mod still restricts irrigated farms to flat desert and
   uses normal Farm art. This source kit makes no changes to that behavior.

No GR2 conversion, game installation, native dynamic adjacency, native terrain
fitting, or live game testing was performed. The requested terrain family is
modeled; universal terrain compatibility is not a property a static mesh alone
can provide. No claim is made of a supported runtime API for arbitrary per-edge
mesh assembly in unmodified Civ V.

REBUILD
Requires Python 3, NumPy and Pillow. No Blender installation is required to
reproduce the sources, viewer, original atlas and review renders.
In the repository:
  python tools/build_irrigation_meshes.py
  python tools/validate_irrigation_meshes.py
From this kit:
  python source/build_irrigation_meshes.py --output rebuilt
  python source/validate_irrigation_meshes.py --output rebuilt
Use --quick on the builder to skip static renders. Validation output is saved
as validation.json. Package contents include the exact generator and validator.
