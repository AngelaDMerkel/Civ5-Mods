# Civilization V: Terrain & Frontier Mods

Plan expeditions through harsh terrain, connect remote resources, and bring water
to desert farms. These mods expand **Civilization V: Brave New World** with new
decisions about where to settle, build and trade.

| Mod | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| [Realistic Terrain](mods/realistic-terrain/README.md) | Environmental exposure, roadside Caravansaries and a new trade planner |
| [Resource Outposts](mods/resource-outposts/README.md) | Defended claims on strategic and luxury resources outside your borders |
| [Irrigated Farm](mods/irrigated-farm/README.md) | Connected desert farms supplied by rivers, mountains, lakes or oases |
| [Climate Change](mods/climate-change/README.md) | A changing landscape over the course of a game — planned |

**In development.** Terrain and Outposts have experimental test builds; irrigation
is currently included with Terrain. Climate Change is not playable yet. Requires
Brave New World and a new single-player game; in-game testing is still pending.
