## Real-world water-system context

[UNESCO: Aflaj Irrigation Systems of Oman](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1207/)
describes gravity-fed channels supplied by groundwater, springs or surface water,
with sluices allocating water to farms. It also describes channels carved into
mountainsides and structures crossing natural obstacles. This supports retaining
a connected gravity system and using plot-boundary distribution, rather than
making every visible channel a monumental aqueduct. The latter is a design
inference, not a quoted architectural prescription.

R5's specific dimensions, asymmetric parcel shapes, reduced retaining heights and
offset drops are authored modeling choices. They are not surveyed from this site.
No web photos or text extracts are bundled as mod artwork. The source page and its
[gallery](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1207/gallery/) remain external references;
individual gallery images retain their credited rights.
