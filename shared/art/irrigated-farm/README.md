# Irrigated Farm concept

The original `irrigated-farm-adjacency-v6.png` and its prompt/notes files were
copied unchanged from the local concept output into this versioned directory.
The Irrigated Farm chat recorded the user's approval before mesh production:
“This is approved. Can you design the mesh for these tiles, making sure you
remain as close to the real Civ5 style as possible”.

The approval covers that concept. The user later requested greater mesh detail
and density; current mesh refinement and native terrain fitting have separate
acceptance. See `approved.json` for source integrity and scope.

On 2026-10-09 the user selected native Farm and Incan Terrace Farm artwork with
readable irrigation additions as the **preferred approach**. See
[native-overlay-study/direction-selection.json](native-overlay-study/direction-selection.json)
for the exact confirmation, referenced study hash and approval scope. This
superseded the replacement-field approach at that time while preserving the v6 concept and
its original approval record. Final mesh acceptance and native validation remain
separate.

[Plot-channel samples](plot-channel-samples-v1/README.md) cover the four established
terrain families with sparse channels between selected crop parcels. These eight
illustrative views retain their exact prompts and provenance; they await review
and do not change the earlier direction selection or certify native mesh fitting.

On 2026-10-20 the user selected v3 as the structural approach and reaffirmed the
native-overlay concept as the visual target, requesting real workable models.
[v3-civ5-direction/selection.json](v3-civ5-direction/selection.json) records both
statements. That newer direction takes precedence over the historical blanket
rejection of v3. The original concepts and their hashes remain unchanged.
Actual candidates are generated by `tools/irrigation_native/build_concept_models.py`;
they require their own design and native-engine acceptance.

On 2026-10-20 the user approved the **R5 source models** with “Approved.”
[v3-civ5-direction/model-approval-r5.json](v3-civ5-direction/model-approval-r5.json)
records the 24-model collection, 72 export hashes and atlas hash. This satisfies
the source-design review prerequisite for R5. The earlier review checkpoints
remain unchanged. Native conversion, terrain fit and in-game acceptance are
pending; earlier floodplain and transition studies are outside this approval.
